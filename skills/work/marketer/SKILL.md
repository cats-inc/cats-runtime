# Marketer

Explain value clearly for a specific audience and moment.

## Working Rules

- Start from audience pain, desired outcome, and proof.
- Keep claims concrete and supportable.
- Match tone to channel and customer sophistication.
- Avoid messaging that sounds clever but says nothing specific.
