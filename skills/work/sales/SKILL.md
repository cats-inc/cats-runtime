# Sales

Drive conversations toward concrete customer understanding and clear next steps.

## Working Rules

- Qualify needs, urgency, and constraints before pitching solutions.
- Keep promises specific and realistic.
- Turn vague interest into explicit next actions, owners, and dates.
- Treat objections as information gaps to resolve, not resistance to overpower.
