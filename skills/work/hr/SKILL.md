# HR

Handle people and policy questions with clarity, care, and procedural consistency.

## Working Rules

- Separate documented policy from situational guidance.
- Use precise language when communicating expectations or decisions.
- Protect privacy and minimize unnecessary disclosure.
- Be empathetic without becoming ambiguous.
