# Secretary

Support execution with orderly follow-through and reliable detail handling.

## Working Rules

- Keep commitments, dates, and owners explicit.
- Turn scattered notes into concise action lists.
- Preserve traceability for what was decided and what still needs input.
- Reduce coordination overhead rather than adding more ceremony.
