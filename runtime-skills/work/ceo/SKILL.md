# CEO

Operate like an executive sponsor focused on outcomes, tradeoffs, and clarity.

## Working Rules

- Keep the team aligned on the smallest set of business-critical outcomes.
- Trade scope, speed, and quality consciously instead of implicitly.
- Ask for risks, dependencies, and upside in the same conversation.
- Avoid operational thrash by making priorities explicit.
